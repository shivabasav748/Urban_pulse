# Demo data specification

**Everything here is synthetic.** None of it is real Belagavi passenger demand, traffic, captain behaviour or flight, train or festival schedules. Real elements are limited to place names and coordinates from OpenStreetMap, dated fuel prices and the sources cited in FARE_ECONOMICS_AND_MARKET_BENCHMARK.md.

## 1. Datasets and where they live
| Dataset | Version | Where | Used by | Kept? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demand history v1 | hgb-v1-seed42 | `model_runs` row only | none (audit) | yes, untouched |
| Demand history v2: 42 days × 8 zones × 48 buckets = 16,128 rows | hgb-v2-seed42 | `demand_observations`, `demand_forecasts`, `src/sim/forecast.json` | live app (zone balance, surge index), Arena | yes, untouched |
| **Demand v3: 365 days × 8 zones × 48 buckets = 140,160 rows** | synthetic-demand-v3, seed 20261113 | `ml/data/v3/demand_v3.csv` (regenerated by `ml/generate_v3.py`, git-ignored) + `metadata_v3.json` | v3 model evaluation (`ml/train_v3.py`, `ml/metrics_v3.json`) | new; **not loaded into the database** |
| Zone-to-zone destination shares | v3 | `ml/data/v3/od_matrix_v3.csv` (256 rows) | documentation and evaluation | new |
| Demo accounts | seed | `auth.users`, `profiles`, `driver_profiles` (17 captains, 5 riders) | live app | yes, untouched |
| Arena fleets | arena-v3 | generated in memory (`src/sim/arena.ts`) | Dispatch Arena, `scripts/arena-study.ts` | n/a |

**Why v3 is not loaded into the app:**
- The app's v2 forecasts are what the database and tests were built and verified on.
- v3 is a richer **evaluation environment**: a full year with seasons, holidays, festivals, events and correlated noise.
- Loading it would replace verified data for a gain the metrics below don't justify for a 3-minute demo.

Loading it later is a forward-only step (new rows tagged `model_version = 'hgb-v3-…'`), and v1/v2 are never overwritten.

## 2. v3 generator (`ml/generate_v3.py`, numpy + pandas, deterministic)
- **Period:** 13 Nov 2025 to 12 Nov 2026 (IST), 30-minute buckets, 8 demand zones (nearest zone centre).
- **Demand:** `demand ~ Poisson(λ)`, with
  `λ = base_zone × Σ purpose weight × purpose curve(hour, workday) × season × rain (×1.3) × festival (×1.15) × exp(day effect + latent) + 0.5 × base × shock`.
- **Zone purposes** come from what each zone contains in OpenStreetMap. The weights are assumptions.

| Zone | Mix |
|---|---|
| Khade Bazar / City Centre | market 45%, commute 20%, station 20%, lunch 15% |
| Tilakwadi / RPD | nightlife 35%, station 25%, college 20%, residential 20% |
| Nehru Nagar / KLE | hospital 40% (KLE Hospital / JNMC), college 30%, commute 30% |
| Vadgaon / Shahapur | residential 70%, market 30% |
| Udyambag Industrial | industrial 80% (shift changes around 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00), commute 20% |
| VTU / Machhe | college 60% (VTU), industrial 40% |
| KSCA Stadium / Ramteerth Nagar | residential 70%, events 30% |
| Belagavi Airport (Sambra) | airport 100%: a **synthetic** two-wave daily pattern, not a real flight schedule |

- **Day type:** weekday vs weekend, plus 14 synthetic public holidays and 4 synthetic festival periods of 2–4 days. These are drawn from the seed and are **not the real calendar**.
- **Season:**
  - a monthly factor (0.90–1.06): a May college-holiday dip, a monsoon dip and a festive uplift (assumption);
  - rain probability by month (Jun 0.55, Jul 0.70, Aug 0.65, Sep 0.45, Oct 0.25, Nov 0.10, otherwise 0.04), with persistence: rain yesterday adds 0.25 (assumption).
- **Correlation:** a day-level AR(1) zone effect (φ = 0.7, σ = 0.06) and a bucket-level AR(1) latent term (φ = 0.85, σ = 0.12). Adjacent buckets are strongly correlated; the test requires r > 0.8.
- **Shocks with recovery:**
  - stadium event 21:30–23:00 (+4.0);
  - airport wave 13:00–14:30 (+2.0);
  - station late-arrival cluster 23:00–24:00 (+1.5);
  - festival evenings in zones 1, 2 and 4 (+1.2).
  The shock term decays by 0.6 per bucket afterwards, giving a recovery period.
- **Destination shares** (`od_matrix_v3.csv`): by time block (morning, midday, evening, night). Neighbours are favoured, with morning flows toward office, industrial and college zones and evening flows toward residential ones. Rows sum to 1 (tested).
- **Provenance on every row:** `synthetic = true`, `dataset_version`, `seed`, `city`, `spatial_resolution`, `temporal_resolution`, `scenario` (normal, rain, scheduled_event or festival), plus `expected_lambda`.
- `metadata_v3.json` adds the generation timestamp, period, row count and a SHA-256 of the content: `b39534ed4e255dda3cf9ef5a429056119039993871a26a3367756df0fd16853a`.

**Invariant tests** (`ml/test_v3.py`, 6/6 pass):
- one row per zone and bucket;
- provenance present;
- non-negative demand;
- byte-identical for the same seed and different for another seed;
- adjacent-bucket correlation above 0.8;
- the industrial zone busier on workdays;
- evenings more than twice as busy as night;
- event buckets more than twice the baseline λ;
- destination rows summing to 1;
- features using only the past, including that the first rows of zone 2 don't borrow zone 1 values.

## 3. v3 model (`ml/train_v3.py`) and v2 audit
**v2 audit:**
- Target 30 minutes after the latest lag; chronological split; seasonal-naive baseline.
- `incident` (unpredictable) is not a feature.
- `rain` and `scheduled_event` are read at target time, which assumes a 30-minute nowcast or a known schedule (documented).
- One defect: the v2 rolling mean isn't grouped by zone, so the first 3 buckets of each zone on day 0 borrow from the previous zone. Those rows are outside the train and test windows (training starts on day 8), so the published v2 metrics are unaffected. Fixed in v3 (tested).

**v3 setup:**
- **Split:** train days 28–300 (104,832 rows), validation 301–336 (13,824; chooses `max_iter` = 400), test 337–364 (10,752). The split is chronological, asserted in code.
- **Features:** zone, hour, day of week, weekend, holiday, festival, rain, scheduled event, month, lags t−1, t−2, t−48 and t−336, a 2-hour rolling mean, and the mean of the same bucket over the previous 28 days.

**Test-window results (synthetic):**

| | MAE | RMSE | MAPE (non-zero actuals) | WAPE | Bias |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **v3 model** | **0.746** | **1.048** | 48.9% | 74.6% | −0.011 |
| Recent average (same bucket, 4 weeks) | 0.777 | 1.094 | 51.4% | 77.7% | +0.025 |
| Last value (t−1) | 0.929 | 1.440 | 74.7% | 92.8% | −0.001 |
| Seasonal naive (same time last week) | 0.958 | 1.493 | 77.4% | 95.8% | +0.008 |
| Same time yesterday | 0.969 | 1.515 | 77.7% | 96.8% | −0.002 |

**Breakdowns:**
- **Peak** (08–10, 17–20): MAE 1.028, bias −0.05 (under-forecasts peaks). **Off-peak:** 0.652.
- **Weekday:** 0.763. **Weekend:** 0.703. **Holiday or festival:** 0.678.
- **Scheduled-event buckets:** 1.330, against 1.886 for seasonal naive (n = 35, small).
- **Rain buckets:** 1.082, against 1.477 for seasonal naive.
- **Per zone, MAE (model vs seasonal naive):** 1 0.98/1.33, 2 0.81/1.05, 3 1.03/1.35, 4 0.83/1.07, 5 0.86/1.11, 6 0.65/0.77, 7 0.45/0.55, 8 0.36/0.45.

**Reading these honestly:**
- The model beats seasonal naive by 22%, but beats a simple **recent-average baseline by only 4%**.
- Demand averages about 1 request per bucket, so percentage errors are large (MAPE is only reported on non-zero actuals; WAPE is the safer measure).
- None of this is evidence of accuracy in the real city.

## 4. Supply and simulation fleets (Arena v3)
- **Fleets:** 28 cabs by default. High-density 60 and low-density 14 scenarios. Stress configurations of **17, 50, 100 and 250** captains with demand at 1×, 2× and 4× (`scripts/arena-study.ts batch`). These are simulation sizes, not claims about Belagavi's fleet.
- **Availability:** about 15% of captains start late (5–40 min) and about 25% take a 20-minute break. "Captain offline" scenarios log 30% off mid-run. Breaks and log-offs fail pending offers, which are then reassigned.
- **Behaviour:** acceptance probability is 0.55–0.95 by default (0.25–0.55 in the many-rejections scenario). Outcomes are accept, reject or no response; a timeout takes 1.5 minutes.
- **Movement:** captains move to the drop-off at completion and to the zone centre after repositioning. Trip time follows distance and the time-of-day speed (20/28 km/h, bikes ×1.15). Captains repositioning are unavailable until they arrive.
- **Rider patience:** 10 minutes by default (4 minutes in the high-cancellation scenario). Queues grow when supply is short and shrink as riders are served or give up.
- **Preference:** a 30% preference share with 45% or 10% of captains opted in. A preference request only ever goes to an opted-in captain (0 violations, tested).

## 5. Live demo database consistency (enforced, not simulated)
| Rule | How it's enforced |
|---|---|
| One captain can't hold overlapping trips | Partial unique index on active trip groups per captain (concurrency 2a–3b) |
| A captain on a trip isn't available | `_rank`, `zone_balance` and availability counts exclude active trips |
| Pending offers are counted correctly | One pending offer per captain and per ride (unique indexes) |
| Positions move plausibly | 150 km/h GPS teleport guard; manual-pin zone arrival must take at least half the estimated drive |
| Zone changes follow physical movement | `op_zone` changes only on observed arrival (SQL G) |
| Women-driver matching uses only opted-in captains | Hard filter plus revalidation under lock (SQL B–D, I; concurrency 5a–5b) |
| Safety ratings only for completed trips | `submit_safety_feedback` checks; unique per ride; none seeded |
| Earnings reconcile with fare and payout | `rideEconomics` unit tests: payout + platform + GST = fare; empty kilometres cost the driver |

## 6. Limitations
- The purpose weights, curves, shocks and seasons are assumptions, not calibrated to real Belagavi data, which isn't available to this project.
- v3 only feeds model evaluation; the live app and Arena still use v2 forecasts.
- Routing is straight-line × 1.35 with two speed levels; there are no road classes or measured congestion.
